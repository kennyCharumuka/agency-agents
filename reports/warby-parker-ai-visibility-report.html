What it says: Warby Parker is the most-recommended DTC eyewear brand in AI answers. It leads its direct online rivals by roughly 2:1 on visibility score, and 5.8K of its own pages are being cited. What it says: AI engines recognize Warby Parker's /learn hub, which carries factual, single-question explainers (OD vs OS, PD, reading a prescription), plus its core category pages. That is the content type LLMs extract most easily. The weaknesses: (1) /learn competes with medical publishers that carry stronger E-E-A-T, so AI often cites them for the "trust" sentence and WP for the "how-to" sentence; (2) commercial and comparison pages (price, insurance, contacts, sunglasses) aren't built as answer-ready content, so price-led rivals take those prompts; (3) the backlink base is shrinking, which erodes the off-site authority that both Google and LLM retrieval reward.

4 · Strategy: Six Actionable Steps

  1. Win the affordability and comparison prompts (content creation). Build answer-first pages for "how much do Warby Parker glasses cost", "Warby Parker vs Zenni / Eyebuydirect / LensCrafters", "best affordable prescription glasses online", "does Warby Parker take my insurance / FSA / HSA". Open each with a 40–60-word direct answer, then add a price and inclusions table, then an FAQ block. State facts LLMs can quote ("from $95 including prescription lenses, free shipping, 30-day returns").
  2. Make /learn the medically authoritative source (E-E-A-T and structured data). Add optometrist bylines and "medically reviewed by OD" stamps with reviewer bio pages and dates. Cite AAO and NIH sources. Add FAQPage, HowTo, MedicalWebPage, Article (author/reviewer) and Organization schema. Refresh the top 30 /learn URLs and build new clusters: progressives, blue light, kids' vision, astigmatism, contact lens fitting.
  3. Build citations in the sources AI engines draw on (authority). Target the third-party pages LLMs cite for eyewear prompts: Reddit (r/glasses, r/optometry), "best online glasses" round-ups (Wirecutter, CNET, Verywell, Healthline), YouTube reviews, and Wikipedia and Wikidata entity accuracy. Run digital PR with proprietary data (e.g., an annual "State of Vision" survey from exam data) to win links back and reverse the −3.9% backlink trend.
  4. Optimize category and product pages for AI and SEO overlap. Add on-page Q&A, Product/Offer/AggregateRating/Review schema, frame-measurement specs and "who it's for" copy to /eyeglasses, /sunglasses and /contacts. Feed Google Merchant Center and ChatGPT and Perplexity shopping feeds with complete attributes so the brand appears in agentic-shopping answers.
  5. Technical AI readiness. Allow GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended in robots.txt. Publish llms.txt pointing to /learn hubs, pricing and policies. Server-render answer content (not JS-only), keep XML sitemaps fresh, and fix Core Web Vitals on PDPs.
  6. Local and entity signals for eye exams. Complete Google Business Profiles for ~300 stores (services, exam booking, insurance accepted) plus Optician/LocalBusiness schema on store pages to compete with LensCrafters for "eye exam near me" in AI Mode and Maps answers.
